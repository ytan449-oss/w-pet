# W Pet visual assets

## Lifestyle hero

`public/images/w-pet-meadow.webp` is AI-generated **concept imagery**, made with the built-in imagegen tool. It does not represent a photographed production product. The homepage labels it “Concept imagery” / “概念图片”. The generated PNG was encoded as WebP without changing its composition. Image size: 1122 × 1402 pixels.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial lifestyle hero photograph for W Pet, an original New Zealand pet brand. Create one portrait-oriented image, approximately 4:5 composition. A happy golden retriever sitting in a natural sunlit grassy garden, wearing a soft cream cotton triangle bandana with a very small delicate sage green and dusty rose botanical flower pattern. Eye-level animal portrait, its full head and chest clearly visible, dog centred with room above its ears. Distant trees, scattered tiny wildflowers, warm late afternoon natural light, candid yet refined editorial photography, film-like subtle grain, soft botanical greens and warm cream colour palette. Beautiful realistic fur, gentle cheerful expression, believable fabric folds. This is concept branding imagery. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no graphic overlays. Generate the actual photo only, not a website screenshot.
